An evening with: The Doctor, The Therapist & The Coach

Our first book covers everything hormones, health and happiness, with a side serving of warmth and humour. Our 3 professional insights into person specific problems gives our highly interactive evening a tri-perspective edge.

From talking about Olympic Cleans to Ovaries and Clitoris, and from Bad Breath to Bad Behaviour - come and let us help you talk about life, from all 3 angles.

An evening with The Doctor, The Therapist and The Coach can help you evaluate your biology, your wellness and your ‘self’. The most informative, honest and real question and answer session ever! Ask us, we will probably know.

The 4th chair: The Doctor, The Therapist & The Coach Coming Soon!

Following on from their first podcast series, The Doctor, The Therapist & The Coach have added a 4th chair to the YO1 Radio studio.

The 4th chair is an invitation to share some time with Angela, Colette and Gemma, to talk about life and all that it brings. Using their honest way of humanising and demystifying their professional roles, they combine their knowledge, skills and selves into a framework for the chair sitter to apply to their own lives.
