Podcast
Thought provoking talks

Pilot: Making Decision

DTC discusses the different ways in which they make decisions, how they help their clients/patients make decisions and why people can find this process challenging with top tips on how to make the right decision for you.

Episode 2: Being Overwhelmed

DTC talks about how we recognise overwhelm, where we can feel it and what we can do about it.

Episode 3: Refusal

DDTC talks about what we do when we feel a strong "NO!'. How to understand why we feel it, how to control the no so that it works for you... and paddle boarding.

Episode 4: Do you need more?

DTC discusses when enough is enough.

Episode 5: The Struggle

DTC talk 'choosing your hard.' Why the struggle is an important and beneficial part of success.

Episode 6: The Change

DTC discusses the importance and benefits of change. Including menopause and unexpected life changing experiences, with advice on how to best deal with changes when they are challenging.

Coming Soon: Menopause part I

DTC talks about menopause - how to recognise it, why it is challenging and how you can educate yourself so you can gain a sense of control.

Coming Soon: Menopause part II

In the second part of this menopause 2 part season finale, DTC discusses more about menopause, management options and support.
