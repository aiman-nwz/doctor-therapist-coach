Meet The Trio
Angela, Colette & Gemma

Dr Angela is a GP, BMS accredited menopause specialist and clinical sexologist. She is passionate about making things better for women, and works with each one of her patients to listen and understand what is happening through a biopsychosocial lens. Using her diverse knowledge, Angela believes educating, not only her clients, but the next generation of specialists is vital for giving women more choices over their health.

Colette is a BACP person centred therapist and business woman. She created her therapeutic space 15 yrs ago dedicated to the wellbeing of her clients. Those looking to find understanding, some clarity and maybe even some change, find a space to explore a more awake, happy and beautiful life.

Gemma is a professional strength athlete winning 4 x Britain’s Strongest Woman, The European Cup and World’s Strongest Woman Runner up twice. She is an advanced performance nutritionist and certified integrative health coach. Creator of the PowerBurn concept, Gemma believes that making positive changes on the body’s inside, mirrors on the body’s outside. Gemma loves finding people’s SuperPowers, and in 2008, she began her mission to help everyday people use them to empower their lives.
