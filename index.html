The Three
Doctor Therpist & Coach

Angela, Colette and Gemma are experts in their respective fields. Having met by chance, they quickly realised that they do the same work from slightly different angles. Unified by a vision of humanising and demystifying the roles they inhabit, they have combined their knowledge, skills and selves into an accessible framework for people to apply to their own lives.


Making the decision to visit a doctor, a therapist or a coach can be life changing, but we know it can also be difficult. We understand the importance of feeling comfortable in your skin and in your life and we are here to support you whatever your needs. We feel stronger together and we would love to help you feel stronger too.
