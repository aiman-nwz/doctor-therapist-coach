# The Doctor, The Therapist & The Coach

A multi-page website representing **The Doctor, The Therapist & The Coach (DTC)** — a collaborative initiative featuring Dr. Angela (Doctor), Colette (Therapist), and Gemma (Coach). The trio combines their professional expertise to demystify wellness, biology, mental health, and physical empowerment through an accessible framework.

## 🌟 About the Project

This website serves as an informational hub for audiences looking to explore health, therapy, nutrition, and personal growth. It connects users with podcast episodes, recommended reading materials, upcoming live events, and direct contact forms.

---

## 📄 Key Features & Pages

- **Home (`index.html`)**: Introduces the DTC mission and philosophy of holistic support across medicine, therapy, and coaching.
- **About (`About.html`)**: Comprehensive profiles of Dr. Angela, Colette, and Gemma, including their respective professional backgrounds and external practices.
- **Books (`Books.html`)**: Curated list of recommended books and audiobooks with direct links to Audible and Amazon.
- **Podcast (`Podcast.html`)**: Directory of podcast episodes hosted on YO1 Radio, discussing topics from decision-making to navigating menopause.
- **Events (`Events.html`)**: Information about upcoming live Q&A sessions, workshops, and studio sessions (e.g., *The 4th Chair*).
- **Contact (`Contact.html`)**: Interactive contact and feedback form alongside newsletter subscription options.

---

## 👥 Meet the Trio

- **Dr. Angela (The Doctor)**: GP, BMS accredited menopause specialist, and clinical sexologist focusing on biopsychosocial wellness.
- **Colette (The Therapist)**: BACP person-centred therapist dedicated to client wellbeing, clarity, and mindfulness.
- **Gemma (The Coach)**: 4x Britain’s Strongest Woman, advanced performance nutritionist, and certified integrative health coach.
